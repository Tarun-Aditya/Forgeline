# Out of scope

Decisions the team already made not to build or change. Intent-writer and
spec-writer read this so a rejected idea is not proposed again. A person adds a line
when they decide; agents may not edit it.

Format: `- <what> (<date>): <reason>`. Delete a line when the decision is reversed.
